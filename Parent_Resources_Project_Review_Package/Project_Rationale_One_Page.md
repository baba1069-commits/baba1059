# Project Rationale — Parkdale Parent Resources Project

## Purpose of the Parent Resources Project

The Parent Resources Project aims to give Parkdale families easy access to reliable, respectful, and accessible information that supports children's sense of belonging, identity, cultural understanding, and family inclusion. The goal is to curate trustworthy resources families can browse on their own, not to provide clinical or legal advice.

## Why LGBTQ+ and Indigenous resources are relevant to family engagement

Parkdale serves a diverse community, including families with gender-diverse children and Indigenous families. Including well-sourced, professionally produced materials on these topics signals to all families that the centre values their identity and experience, and gives parents accessible starting points for conversations they may not otherwise have support for. This directly supports children's sense of belonging and family engagement — core goals of early childhood education.

## Why supervisor review is required

These topics can be sensitive, and the resource-selection process must prioritize accuracy, age-appropriateness, and fit with Parkdale's specific community before anything is displayed. As a student intern, my role is to research, screen, and organize candidate resources — not to make final content decisions for the parent resource area. Anna's review ensures professional oversight, consistency with centre policy, and an additional check against any content that may need adjustment before reaching families.

## Connection to inclusion, belonging, cultural responsiveness, and professional responsibility

This project reflects core RECE practice: working from official/professional sources rather than assumptions, respecting families' diverse identities and cultures, and recognizing the limits of a student intern's authority to make final placement decisions. Submitting this package for supervisor approval — rather than printing and displaying materials independently — demonstrates professional accountability and respect for the supervised nature of the internship.
