# First Nations Child & Family Caring Society — Spirit Bear

- Resource title: Spirit Bear (children's books, calendar, Spirit Bear Plan)
- Source organization: First Nations Child & Family Caring Society ("the Caring Society")
- Link: https://fncaringsociety.com/SpiritBear (Books & Learning Guides: https://fncaringsociety.com/spirit-bear/books-learning-guides)
- Topic category: Child-friendly Books and Family-friendly Information Sheets / Reconciliation
- Why it may support Parkdale families: Spirit Bear is an award-winning picture-book series (by Cindy Blackstock, illustrated by Spotted Fawn Productions) that introduces reconciliation themes to young children in an age-appropriate way. Free digital copies are available, and books exist in French and some First Nations languages — well suited to an early-childhood setting.
- Suggested use: Print/binder (book covers + a short description) or QR code linking directly to the free digital books — this is the most directly "child-friendly" resource collected in this round.
- Notes about sensitivity or supervisor approval: Some books touch on Residential Schools and the Truth and Reconciliation Commission in age-appropriate ways — Anna should confirm tone/age-appropriateness for Parkdale's specific age group before display.
