## Program: Psychology (Developmental and Communication Science) — MASc
- Program name: MASc in Psychology, Developmental and Communication Science (DCS) field
- Degree type: MASc (note: not MA/MEd; one-year program)
- Department/Faculty: Department of Psychology, Faculty of Arts, University of Waterloo
- Program URL: https://uwaterloo.ca/future-graduate-students/programs/by-faculty/arts/psychology-developmental-and-communication-science-master ; https://uwaterloo.ca/psychology/masc-program-developmental-communication-science-dcs
- Minimum GPA: 80% (or equivalent) average in final two years of undergraduate study
- Required degree background: Bachelor's degree
- Language requirement (IELTS/TOEFL): TOEFL overall 4.5-scale (writing 5 / speaking 5 on a 4-section scale); IELTS overall 7.0 (writing 6.5 / speaking 6.5)
- Number of reference letters required: 3 academic references
- GRE/other test required: Not listed as mandatory for the Developmental Psychology stream (only the I/O Psychology stream requires GRE per official page) — whether DCS is exempt needs official verification
- Statement of purpose / research proposal required: Yes — Supplementary Information Form (SIF) plus a separate Personal Statement (≤2000 words) describing research interest and intended supervisor, in lieu of a traditional SOP
- Application deadline: December 1 (for September intake of the following year)
- Funding/scholarship notes: Program page states a guaranteed funding package of approximately $18,000/year (including 2 TA positions) for the 1-year MASc
- Important caveat: Official page explicitly states international-student spots are "extremely limited due to budget constraints" — this materially affects application strategy and should be confirmed directly with the department.
