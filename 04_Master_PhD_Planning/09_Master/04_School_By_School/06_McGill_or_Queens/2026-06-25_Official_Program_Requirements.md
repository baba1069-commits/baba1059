## McGill University
### Program: MA Education and Society (Non-Thesis), Department of Integrated Studies in Education (DISE)
(Note: McGill also has more specialized entry points such as First Nations and Inuit Education and a Disability Studies certificate within DISE, but only this core MA's requirements were verified this round.)
- Program name: MA Education and Society (Non-Thesis option; Thesis option also exists)
- Degree type: MA
- Department/Faculty: Department of Integrated Studies in Education (DISE), Faculty of Education, McGill University
- Program URL: https://www.mcgill.ca/gradapplicants/program/education-and-society-ma-non-thesis ; https://www.mcgill.ca/education/programs/masters/master-arts-programs/master-arts-education-and-society
- Minimum GPA: CGPA 3.0/4.0, or 3.2/4.0 over the most recent two years of full-time study
- Required degree background: Bachelor's degree
- Language requirement (IELTS/TOEFL): IELTS overall 7.0 with writing ≥7.0; TOEFL iBT overall 92, writing/speaking ≥22, reading/listening ≥20
- Number of reference letters required: 2 (at least 1 from a university instructor)
- GRE/other test required: Needs official verification (not mentioned on accessible pages)
- Statement of purpose / research proposal required: Yes — 1–2 page Personal Statement / Letter of Intent
- Application deadline: January 1 for Fall intake
- Funding/scholarship notes: DISE funding page indicates full-time Thesis MA/PhD students may apply for DISE funding; Non-Thesis (course-based) students are not eligible. Specific amounts: needs official verification.

## Queen's University
### Program: Master of Education (MEd), on-campus
(Note: Queen's also has a dedicated Indigenous-focused entry point, the WISE program, not separately verified this round.)
- Program name: Master of Education (MEd), on-campus
- Degree type: MEd
- Department/Faculty: Faculty of Education, Queen's University
- Program URL: https://educ.queensu.ca/graduate/on-campus/masters ; https://www.queensu.ca/academic-calendar/graduate-studies/programs-study/education/education-med/
- Minimum GPA: Needs official verification — official page references "second-class standing" without a confirmed GPA-equivalent number
- Required degree background: Bachelor's degree
- Language requirement (IELTS/TOEFL): IELTS minimum Band 7.0; TOEFL 580 (paper) / 237 (computer) / 92 (internet-based), TWE recommended ≥4.5
- Number of reference letters required: Needs official verification
- GRE/other test required: Needs official verification
- Statement of purpose / research proposal required: Yes — applicants must state research interests and program fit in the online application
- Application deadline: January 15 for complete application (transcripts + references) for Fall intake; domestic applicants may have until April 15 if spots remain (not applicable to international applicants)
- Funding/scholarship notes: Page mentions a competitive funding package, travel grants, and TA opportunities; specific amounts need official verification.
