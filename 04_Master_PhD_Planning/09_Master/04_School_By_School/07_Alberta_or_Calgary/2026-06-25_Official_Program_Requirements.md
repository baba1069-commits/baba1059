## University of Alberta — Faculty of Education
### Program: MEd in Elementary Education
(Relevant to Early Childhood Education / ECE leadership interest.)
- Program name: Master of Education (MEd) in Elementary Education
- Degree type: MEd
- Department/Faculty: Faculty of Education, University of Alberta
- Program URL: https://www.ualberta.ca/en/graduate-programs/master-of-education-elementary-education.html ; https://www.ualberta.ca/en/elementary-education/graduate-programs/admissions-and-deadlines.html

- Statement of purpose / research proposal required: Yes — Statement of Intent (~500 words) plus CV
- Application deadline: Needs official verification
- Funding/scholarship notes: Faculty/university-level awards available (e.g. M.E. Lazerte Memorial Graduate Scholarship); University of Alberta Graduate Entrance Scholarship ($17,500 for Master's level)
