# Kalchos, Kassan, & Ford (2022) — Access to Support Services for Newcomer Youth

- **Authors:** Kalchos, [[Anusha_Kassan]], & [[Laurie_Ford]]
- **Citation:** Kalchos, Kassan, & Ford (2022). "Access to Support Services for Newcomer Youth Through the Process of School Integration: A Critical Narrative Literature Review."

## Summary

A critical narrative literature review examining how newcomer youth access support services during the process of school integration. Co-authored by two UBC ECPS faculty — [[Anusha_Kassan]] (counselling psychology, migration/social-justice) and [[Laurie_Ford]] (child development, ECE Program Director) — making it the strongest direct evidence link between these two professors in this vault.
