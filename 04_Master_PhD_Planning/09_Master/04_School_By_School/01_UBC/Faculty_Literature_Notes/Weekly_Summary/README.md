# UBC Weekly Faculty Research Summary

This subfolder holds weekly roll-ups of that week's UBC Faculty Literature Notes — a short aggregation of what was read that week, not a full re-research or a new literature review.

## Naming convention

`YYYY-MM-DD_to_YYYY-MM-DD_UBC_Weekly_Faculty_Research_Summary.md` (start date to end date of the week covered).

## Cadence rule

- Weekly only — one summary per week, built from that week's `../*_Literature_Note.md` files (typically 2–3 of them, per the 2–3-times-a-week cadence).
- Summarize, do not re-research: pull the "why this matters for Jiecheng" and "open questions" sections from each note rather than re-reading the underlying publications.
- Prioritize relevance to Jiecheng's ECE, inclusive education, child development, Indigenous/LGBTQ+ family resources, and Master's application pathway when deciding what to highlight.

## Scope

- Allowed: summary markdown files aggregating that week's literature notes (themes covered, open questions, recommended next professor/publication to read).
- Not allowed: private documents, identification documents, receipts, OSAP/financial-aid files, immigration documents, or original internship evidence.
