## Publication examined

**Keenan, H. B. (2017). "Unscripting Curriculum: Toward a Critical Trans Pedagogy." *Harvard Educational Review*, 87(4), 538–556.**

Source: Harvard Educational Review official page (harvardeducationalreview.org/content/87/4/538) and ERIC (EJ1164883).

## What the article actually argues (from the official abstract)

Keenan draws on his own experience as a white queer and trans educator and former elementary school teacher to examine what a "critical trans pedagogy" means in practice. He explores how his own classroom teaching was shaped both by his personal experience of gender conditioning and by the political climate around trans identity at the time. His central claim: a critical trans pedagogy requires **"unscripting"** — moving away from pre-set, adult-determined narratives about gender — and must actively support children in constructing their own knowledge about gender, rather than simply receiving adult-approved narratives.
