# UBC Faculty Literature Notes

This folder holds deeper, single-professor reading notes that go one level beyond the summary entries in `../2026-06-25_Faculty_Research_Map.md`.

## Cadence rule

- **Frequency:** 2–3 times per week, not daily. Do not create a heavy UBC literature review every day.
- **Scope per note:** Exactly **one professor at a time**. Do not batch multiple professors into one note.
- **Priority order:** Pick whichever professor in the Faculty Research Map is flagged "Worth deeper reading later: Yes — high priority" first, then work down the list. Prioritize relevance to Jiecheng's ECE, inclusive education, child development, Indigenous/LGBTQ+ family resources, and Master's application pathway over completeness.
- **Do not over-collect:** one representative publication or project per note is enough depth; flag anything else as "Needs Verification" rather than chasing a full bibliography.

## Naming convention

`YYYY-MM-DD_ProfessorLastName_Literature_Note.md`

## Scope

- Allowed: reading notes on a single professor's specific publication(s)/project(s), drawn from accessible official or academic sources (journal pages, university news, ERIC, Google Scholar, ResearchGate). Mark anything unconfirmed "Needs Verification."
- Not allowed: contacting professors directly; inventing publication titles, findings, or admission/supervision claims; private documents, IDs, receipts, OSAP/immigration files, or internship evidence.
